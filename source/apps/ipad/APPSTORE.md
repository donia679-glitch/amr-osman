## 2. Before "Submit for Review"
1. Apple Developer Program (individual is fastest; a company account needs a D-U-N-S number).
2. App Store Connect → new app, bundle ID `com.novera.studio`, name "NOVERA Kitchen Studio".
3. Subscriptions → group "NOVERA Kitchen Studio" → `novera.studio.monthly`, `novera.studio.yearly`, each with an introductory offer = free trial 3 days, names/descriptions in Arabic + English.
4. `ios/Store.swift`: put the Subscription Group ID, set `required = true`.
5. Fill the support email/phone in `legal/privacy.html` and publish it (Privacy Policy URL + Support URL).
6. Build from Swift Playgrounds (or Xcode) → Upload to App Store Connect.
7. Screenshots, App Privacy, age rating, export compliance, Review Notes → Submit.

## 4. Listing — English

**Promotional text:** Design a kitchen or wardrobe on your iPad and get the cutting plan, labels, assembly guide and quotation in minutes.
**Keywords:** kitchen,cabinet,wardrobe,furniture,carpentry,cutlist,cutting,sheet,panel,labels,CNC,woodwork
**Description:**
NOVERA Kitchen Studio is the app for furniture factories and workshops: it designs, calculates and organizes the work from the site survey to the last assembled part.

Design
• Kitchens (base, wall, tall, corner, oven, fridge, washer, sink), wardrobes, bedrooms, TV units and tables.
• Drawing Studio: draw any part or unit from scratch in 3D — lines, rectangles, circles, arcs, push/pull, cut-outs, pockets, rounded corners, copy and rotate.
• Every manufacturing setting: board thickness, back and groove, plinth and plinth drawer, cam-lock fittings, hinge cups, drawer boxes, edge banding.

Survey and room
• Step-by-step site survey, or scan the room with the camera (LiDAR).
• Doors, windows, columns and electrical, plumbing and gas points with their heights.

Manufacture
• Cutting plan with minimal waste and a fixed number for every part on the label, barcode and assembly guide.
• PDF labels with QR and barcode, and a CNC file for every part in its real shape.
• Price every unit from the area of its own parts, and send a PDF quotation to your client.

Share
• Excel, CSV, PDF, DXF, images and a 3D model (DAE).

## 6. Review Notes (paste in App Store Connect)
NOVERA Kitchen Studio is a professional design-and-manufacturing tool for kitchen and furniture workshops. The full app is bundled and runs offline (no account, no server). Native features: Apple RoomPlan room scanning (LiDAR devices), AR Quick Look to place the design in the room, StoreKit subscription with Restore Purchases, and the share sheet for PDF / Excel / DXF exports.
To try it: tap "Continue «Sample project»" on the home screen — a sample kitchen opens. Steps along the top: Room → Design → Materials → Cutting → Parts → Price → Client → Workshop. ☰ Menu → Language switches Arabic / English.
The subscription unlocks the app; please use a Sandbox account.
